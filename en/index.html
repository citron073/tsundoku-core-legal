Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

TSUNDOKU CORE ("the App") is operated by ALTER CORE ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information the App handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Operator: ALTER CORE, a sole proprietorship based in Japan
Contact: altercore073@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

The App stores the following information using Supabase (a database service).

Account information

  • If you use Sign in with Apple: your name and email address. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only the relay address Apple creates.
  • If you use Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture.

We never receive your password. Apple and Google handle sign-in and tell us only the result.

Information you add

  • The books you add and what you enter about them (title, author, ISBN, page count, price paid, purchase date, reading status, notes, tags and location)
  • Photos you take of covers and spines
  • Reading actions, projects and authors you follow
  • Feedback you send us

Device information

  • A push notification token, only if you turn on notifications for possible new releases

We do not collect your location, contacts, whole photo library or advertising identifier (IDFA). The camera is used only to scan barcodes and to photograph covers and spines.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To save and show your library
  • To show your unread pile, related books, what to read next and your badges
  • To check for and tell you about possible new releases by authors you follow
  • To answer your messages and to find and fix problems

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train or run generative AI.

4. What other users can see

Photos of covers and spines

Anyone who has the exact address (URL) of a photo you take can view it, because photos are stored in a publicly readable location. The addresses are made of hard-to-guess identifiers, and there is no way to browse other people's photos in the App, but we cannot guarantee that only you can see them. Please make sure nothing but the book (such as your surroundings or handwriting) appears in the photo.

Only you can add or delete your photos. Deleting a book or your account also deletes its photos.

Shared book details

Book details such as ISBN, title, author, publisher, page count, size (height) and list price are stored as data shared by all users. If you fill in missing details in the "Book details" section of the Edit screen, other users who have the same book will see them too. This avoids fetching the same book's details again and again.

Your price paid, purchase date, reading status, notes, tags and location belong only to you and are never shared with other users.

Friends

You can send a friend request by entering someone's exact email address. Badges and finished counts are shared only with friends who have both accepted. Nothing is shown to anyone else or to the public, and there are no rankings.

5. Service providers

We do not sell your personal information or give it to third parties without your consent. We use these service providers to run the App:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, delivering push notifications)
  • Google (Google sign-in)
  • Expo (relaying push notifications)
  • Resend (emailing us the feedback you send, with your user ID, so we can read it)

Book details come from openBD, the National Diet Library of Japan (NDL Search) and Open Library. We send them only an ISBN or an author's name, never information that identifies you.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea. Some of our service providers are based in the United States (Supabase, Apple, Google, Expo and Resend). Your information may therefore be processed outside your country, where privacy laws may differ.

7. How we protect it

  • All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database controls access per user, so no one can read or change another user's personal data (except the shared information described in section 4).
  • Administrative credentials are kept only by us and are not included in the App.
  • We collect only what the App's features need (section 2).

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account yourself at any time from Settings → Delete account in the App. This deletes your account, your book records, the photos you took and your feedback. It cannot be undone. It may take some time for the deletion to be fully reflected in our systems. Emails that told us about your feedback (sent through Resend, section 5) stay in our mailbox as a record of our reply; contact us (section 9) if you want those deleted too.

The shared book details described in section 4 (ISBN, title, author, page count and so on) are not deleted, because they describe the book, not you, and other users rely on them.

Before deleting, you can export your books at any time from Settings → Export your library as CSV (free). If we ever end the service, we will announce it in advance and give you time to export your data.

9. Your choices and rights

You can view, correct, export and delete your information in the App at any time. You can also ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop using it, by sending a message from Settings → Send feedback in the App (which lets us confirm it comes from your signed-in account) or by emailing altercore073@gmail.com. We will confirm it is you and respond without undue delay, free of charge. You can turn off notifications at any time in the App or in your device settings.

10. Advertising, tracking and "Do Not Track"

The App shows no ads, and it does not track you across other apps or websites or share usage data with third parties. Because we do not track you, the App does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date on this page. We will tell you in the App about any important change.

13. Contact

For questions or complaints about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us below. You can also send us a message from Settings → Send feedback in the App.
